Before you call

Have these ready

To protect your account, Sofia asks for three details from the same order before showing or changing anything. Questions about our policies need none of them.

  • The phone number on your order
  • Your order number (ORD- followed by six digits)
  • The postal code on your order
  • The product ID, printed on your confirmation and on the box
Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

How long does a return take?

About 3 to 5 business days after we receive your item, then 5 to 7 business days for the refund.

Can I cancel a claim?

Yes, free of charge, while its status is “Awaiting return”. Once the item is under review, ask for a human agent.

Where do I find my order number?

It starts with ORD- followed by six digits. You will find it in your confirmation email, on the packing slip in the box, and in the Orders section of your account.

Can I talk to a person?

Yes. Ask for a human agent at any time. You do not need to verify your identity first.
